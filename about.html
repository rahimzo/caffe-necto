Our Story

The people behind Caffè Necto

Where Every Cup Connects Us

Founded in 2015, Caffè Necto began as a dream to create a space where coffee brings people together. Our founder, Maria Rodriguez, envisioned a café that wasn't just about great coffee, but about the connections made over each cup.

Today, we continue that tradition by sourcing the finest beans, crafting delicious drinks with care, and providing a welcoming space for our community to gather, work, and connect.
